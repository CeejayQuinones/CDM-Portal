AI Academic Monitoring diagrams

CDM Portal, monitoring module only. Scope is early warning from every graded score that makes the student’s grades, study support from a student’s own file, and plans or notices sent to that same student. Source: monitoring routes, EarlyWarningService weights, and the monitoring tables.

1. System flowchart

Start / end Process AI generation Decision

The professor records every graded score and is the only one who sends a study plan or a risk notice. The registrar does not teach. The registrar checks the grade the professor submitted and approves it onto the official record. Monitoring uses that grade only after approval. Admin views the official list and does not approve grades or send plans.

2. Entity-relationship diagram

AI monitoring module only. The four roles are Student, Professor, Registrar staff, and Admin. Admission, enrollment, document requests, and account management are outside this diagram. 1 means one. M means many.

Student, Professor, Registrar staff, and Admin each have one login. The professor submits the grade and records every graded score. The registrar staff approves the grade. Admin views the list and stores nothing. Monitoring reads a grade only when its status is approved. Graded score, Study file, Sent plan, and Risk notice are stored by AI monitoring. A study file is not a graded score. A grade plan can leave the file link empty.

3. Data flow diagram — Level 0

AI monitoring module only. The circle is that module, not the rest of the portal. The professor, the student, and the registrar are separate. Admin only views the official list.

4. Data flow diagram — Level 1

This opens the AI monitoring circle only. Professor records every graded score that helps make the grade, and is the only one who sends.

1.0 reads a period grade only after the registrar has approved it, plus every other graded score the professor recorded. 2.0 stores the student’s own file and does not change the score. 2.1 is the professor, not the registrar. 3.0 shows a draft to the professor. Only 4.0, done by the professor, saves a sent plan or a risk notice. The registrar approves grades and does not send. Admin only views the official list.

5. Data flow diagram — Level 2

This opens process 3.0 inside AI monitoring. The practice quiz is a study aid. It is not one of the graded scores that make the student’s grade.

3.2, 3.3, and 3.4 each read the student’s file on their own. They stop if the text is shorter than 40 characters. None of them produces the next one, and none of them changes the grade or the risk score. 3.5 reads the graded scores and 3.6 turns them into a study plan for the professor. The registrar is not in this diagram. The professor is the only role that can save that plan, or a risk notice, for the student.
